=== NETWORK ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW ===

Network Architecture Requirements:
- Segmented and isolated Staff, Public Service, and Guest user networks via VLANs/SVIs.
- Enforced role-based, MFA-protected management access using AAA, TACACS+/RADIUS, and certificate-based auth.
- Integrated firewall module with advanced L3/L4/L7 ACL, threat detection (IPS), and web filtering policies.
- End-to-end AES-256 encrypted communications: SSHv2, TLS 1.3, IPSec, SNMPv3.
- Full IPv4/IPv6 dual-stack support, routed WAN handoff with static IP.
- Government-controlled NTP, DNS.
- Automated configuration backup, monitoring, logging, NetFlow, and SIEM integration.
- Production HA features: HSRP, LACP, redundant control-plane and power, storm control.
- Full compliance with FISMA, NIST SP 800-53/171, ISO/IEC 27001/27017, CMMC, GDPR, and national telecom/defense/5G/critical infrastructure frameworks.
